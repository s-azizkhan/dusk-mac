Flicker-free dimming for MacBooks.

Many MacBooks dim with PWM, switching the backlight on and off very fast. Dusk keeps the backlight at full power and dims in software.

Backlight pulses on and off hundreds of times a second at low brightness.

What Dusk does.

Blue light filter

From 6500K daylight down to a deep 500K red.

Flicker-free mode

Backlight held at 100%, brightness lowered in software.

No dithering

Turns off temporal dithering on Apple silicon, so the image stops shimmering.

Dim below minimum

Go darker than the brightness keys allow, down to 5%.

Night schedule

Warms on its own during the hours you choose.

Seven presets

Energy, Daylight, Evening, Night, Late, Deep and Midnight.

FAQ

What is Dusk?

A free, open-source macOS menu-bar app. It filters blue light (500K to 6500K), dims below minimum brightness, reduces PWM flicker and turns off temporal dithering.

Is there a free alternative to f.lux or Sundown for Mac?

Yes. Dusk is free and open source (MIT), with a 500K blue light filter, dimming below minimum brightness, flicker-free mode and anti-dithering.

How do I reduce PWM flicker on a MacBook?

Turn on Flicker-free in Dusk. The backlight stays at 100% and brightness is lowered in software through the gamma table.

How do I disable temporal dithering on Apple silicon?

Open Display Health in Dusk and turn on No Dithering. Check it with ioreg -lw0 | grep -i enableDither.

How is Dusk different from Night Shift?

Night Shift stops around 2700K. Dusk goes to 500K, dims to 5% and adds flicker-free and anti-dithering modes.

Does Dusk help with sleep or eye strain?

Dusk is a display utility, not a medical device. Many people find a warmer, dimmer screen more comfortable at night.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

This early build is not notarised yet. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dusk.app once, then open it normally.

Does Dusk collect data?

No. No network requests, no analytics. The full source is on GitHub.
