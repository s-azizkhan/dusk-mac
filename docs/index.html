Install in a minute

1. Download Dusk-macOS.zip from the latest release
2. Move Dusk.app to Applications
3. xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dusk.app
4. Open Dusk. The sun appears in your menu bar.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to f.lux or Sundown for Mac?

Yes. Dusk is free and open source (MIT), with a 500K blue light filter, dimming below minimum brightness, flicker-free mode and anti-dithering.

How do I reduce PWM flicker on a MacBook?

Turn on Flicker-free in Dusk. The backlight stays at 100% and brightness is lowered in software through the gamma table.

How do I disable temporal dithering on Apple silicon?

Open Display health in Dusk and turn on No dithering. Check with ioreg -lw0 | grep -i enableDither.

How is Dusk different from Night Shift?

Night Shift stops around 2700K. Dusk goes to 500K, dims to 5% and adds flicker-free and anti-dithering modes.

Does Dusk help with sleep or eye strain?

Dusk is a display utility, not a medical device. Many people find a warmer, dimmer screen more comfortable at night.

Does Dusk collect data?

No. No network requests, no analytics. Read the source on GitHub.
